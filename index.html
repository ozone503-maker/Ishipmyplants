Frequently asked questions

What is the Punahele Plant Market?

Punahele is a virtual farmers market and plant outlet at FlashTown in Eden Roc, Hawaiʻi. It's run as a neighborhood coop — every plant on the shelf was grown by a neighbor right here in Eden Roc, and the whole market lives online under the name I Ship My Plants.

Who grows the plants at Punahele?

Four Eden Roc growers stock the market shelf: Rooster Island, Tropical Ts, Eden Nova, and FlashTown. Instead of one nursery importing plants, the neighborhood grows them — that's the coop model behind the market.

Where are the plants grown?

Every plant is grown in Eden Roc, a subdivision on Hawaiʻi's Big Island. The market itself is at FlashTown, and the growers are all neighbors in the same community.

What kinds of plants does Punahele sell?

The catalog carries 67 plants in six categories: Native Hawaiian, Tropical Flowers, Fruit Trees, Herbs & Edibles, Foliage, and Palms. You can filter the market shelf by category to browse just one kind at a time.

What native Hawaiian plants does Punahele carry?

Fourteen natives, including ʻŌhiʻa Lehua, Koa, Māmane, Naupaka Kahakai, ʻIlima, ʻAʻaliʻi, ʻŌhelo, Hāpuʻu, Hala, Loulu, and Kī (ti leaf). Planting natives is a way to restore the islands — ʻōhiʻa blossoms, for example, feed native birds.

What fruit trees does Punahele carry?

Fourteen fruit trees, including Lilikoi (passion fruit), Papaya, Guava, Mango, Lychee, Rambutan, Ulu (breadfruit), Mountain Apple, Maiʻa (banana), Pineapple, Niu (coconut), and Starfruit. Ulu is the classic canoe-plant staple — one tree can feed a family.

What tropical flowers does Punahele carry?

Thirteen flowering plants, including Red Anthurium, Red Hibiscus, Pink Plumeria, Bird of Paradise, Torch Ginger, Heliconia, Dendrobium Orchid, Passion Flower, and Bougainvillea. These are the blooms Hawaiʻi is known for, grown by Eden Roc neighbors.

What herbs and edible plants does Punahele carry?

Ten herbs and edibles, including Hawaiian Mint, Kalo (taro), ʻŌlena (turmeric), Ginger, Lemongrass, Basil, Moringa, ʻAwapuhi (shampoo ginger), Roselle, and Okinawan Spinach. Kalo is the root of Hawaiian food — poi, kūlolo, and lūʻau leaf all come from it.

What foliage plants and houseplants does Punahele carry?

Ten foliage plants, including Monstera Deliciosa, Song of India, Croton, ZZ Plant, Pothos, Red Ti Leaf, Calathea, and Elephant Ear. Most are shade-friendly tropicals that work indoors or on a lānai.

What palms does Punahele carry?

Six palms: Areca Palm, Foxtail Palm, Traveler's Palm, Manila Palm, Kentia Palm, and Bismarck Palm. Hawaiʻi's own native palm, Loulu, is listed with the native plants.

How much do plants cost at Punahele?

Catalog prices run from $12 to $65, depending on the plant. Each listing on the market shelf shows its price right on the card, so you can browse and compare before anything goes in your cart.

Can I buy plants from Punahele right now?

You can browse the full catalog and fill a cart today, but checkout isn't connected yet — the cart button reads "Checkout (coming soon)." Online ordering opens once checkout goes live; until then, the shelf is open for looking.

Does Punahele ship plants?

Shipping is the whole idea — the market runs under the name I Ship My Plants. That said, online checkout isn't live yet, so shipping details will be published when ordering opens rather than promised before they exist.

What is the Yard Visualizer?

The Yard Visualizer lets you see a plant in your own space before you buy. Upload a photo of your yard or house, pick a plant, click where it should go, then drag it around and resize it with the slider until it looks right.

How do I use the Yard Visualizer?

Upload a yard photo, choose a plant from the palette, and click the spot where you'd plant it. You can drag plants to move them, use the size slider to scale them, remove one at a time, or clear the whole layout and start over.

What is the Plant ID tool?

Plant ID is a free tool built into the market: upload a photo of a plant and it will try to identify it for you. It works whether the plant came from Punahele or you just found it growing somewhere and want to know what it is.

Where can I find care information for a plant?

Every listing on the market shelf includes the basics: care level, water needs, sun needs, and mature height. Open any plant's card to see what it wants before you decide.

Are the plants at Punahele imported or locally grown?

Locally grown, every one. The coop page says it plainly: every plant on the shelf was grown by a neighbor in Eden Roc — nothing is shipped in from off-island nurseries to be resold.

What is FlashTown?

FlashTown is the home of the market in Eden Roc, on the Big Island — the farm and sanctuary where Punahele is based. It's one of the four growers stocking the shelf, alongside Rooster Island, Tropical Ts, and Eden Nova.

Is Punahele the same as I Ship My Plants?

Yes — same market, two names. Punahele Plant Market at FlashTown is the market itself; I Ship My Plants is the name it runs under online, because shipping plants to your door is the point.
